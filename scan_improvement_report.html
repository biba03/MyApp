# Shoryuken: improvements supported by the backtest

Install: back up your project, extract the Python files, notebook and tests into its root, preserving relative paths, and restart the notebook kernel. This archive contains only changed files and evidence. It contains no market database. Open scan_improvement_report.html independently in your browser.

## What changed
- Next-open orders use the prior available market regime; entry-day closes cannot decide earlier open orders.
- ATR stops check the entry-day close after next-open entry. The 1 ATR rule and next-open exit policy stay unchanged.
- Close exits free capital after open entries; same-day terminal trades pay both commissions. Close-entry orders retain their appropriate close ordering.
- Simulation stops at the configured end date, retaining earlier warmup history. Terminal cash, equity and actual position counts reconcile.
- Funded trade win/loss statistics use actual flat-fee P/L.
- Reports distinguish RawDetectorScore from the existing live composite Score. No live selection weights or detection rules change.
- Notebook backtest uses 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31, 10 positions, 1 ATR, daily steps and existing sector limits.

## Results and interpretation
The earlier fee-reconciled result was +89.97% with -10.97% drawdown. Correcting execution accounting yields +58.37% with -16.66% drawdown, final equity $158,365.09, 43 trades, 23.26% winners and no remaining positions. This is a correction to evidence, not an increase in strategy profitability.

The earlier ANF PocketPivot winner contributed about 38% of net profit; five winners exceeded total net profit. That small concentrated sample does not justify boosting its weight. Comparing only closed 2023 trades would also exclude still-open winners and bias conclusions.

A diagnostic compared raw scores with within-pattern percentiles fitted on training scores only, retaining the existing three anchored fold boundaries and fixed candidates. Returns (raw / percentile): fold 1 15.326% / 3.767%; fold 2 0.356% / 2.842%; fold 3 6.328% / 7.592%. Drawdowns: fold 1 -11.843% / -10.530%; fold 2 -6.856% / -5.580%; fold 3 -8.844% / -9.303%. The criterion required higher return without worse drawdown in at least two folds. Only one passed: normalization was rejected and is not enabled.

## Verification and scope
264 offline test checks passed. A historical sample of 111 live detections retained identical scores, detections and trade levels. Database SHA256 stayed 5fb0669c68e1232fdc568571465060ce500a4130c4164052b0fbbe817f69ade9 (see validation.json for authoritative digest).
Saved signals were reused to regenerate candidate exits; indicators/detectors were not rescanned for the portfolio experiment. No RNG was used. Seeds, fold boundaries, scoring thresholds, portfolio construction, risk settings, historical data length and bootstrap counts were not changed. The offline harness simulates pytest fixtures and blocks network calls; it is not a run under installed pytest.

Existing candidate artifacts lacking exit_at_close retain a legacy fallback: regenerate exits from saved signals or run a fresh backtest to get fully corrected execution. Do not just replay old trade records and assume all corrections apply.

Limitations: this uses the cached stock universe and available sector classifications, so survivorship and classification history remain limitations. No point-in-time market-cap snapshot was supplied: the historical experiment does not validate the live Watchable filter. It does not independently validate KQ/MM/Stockbee strategies, optimize live composite weights, or certify all other research replay engines. Next work should validate the actual live composite on untouched forward data before changing weights.
